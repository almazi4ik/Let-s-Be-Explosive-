💥 Let's Be Explosive!
Explosions, reimagined. Layer by Layer.

Tired of ordinary TNT explosions? Let's Be Explosive! brings a fresh approach to Minecraft explosions!

Instead of destroying blocks all at once, explosions progress gradually, destroying the world layer by layer.

Experience a new level of destruction with material-based resistance, entity damage and progressive block destruction.

✨ Features
Layer-by-Layer Destruction — Explosions destroy blocks progressively, one layer at a time.

Material Resistance — Different materials have different levels of resistance to explosions.

Progressive Damage — Nearby living entities take damage as each layer is destroyed.

Block Drops — Destroyed blocks can drop their items.

Visual Effects — Particles accompany the destruction.

🔮 Future Plans
More features, improvements and Minecraft version support are planned for the future!
